Log in with Twitch

Twitch gives every streaming program its own “Client ID”. Getting one is free and takes two minutes, once:

  1. Open dev.twitch.tv/console/apps in your browser and log in.
  2. “Register Your Application”: any name, OAuth Redirect URL http://localhost, Category Broadcaster Suite, Client Type Public.
  3. Open the new application (“Manage”) and copy its Client ID here.
Or paste a stream key instead

On twitch.tv: Creator Dashboard, Settings, Stream, “Primary Stream key”. It is stored encrypted on this computer and never shown again.

Advanced

MyStream sends the picture the way browsers do (WebRTC, “WHIP”). Twitch accepts that but still calls it experimental.

Tip: Steam can draw its pop-ups inside a game’s own window. To keep them off the stream, switch off “Enable the Steam Overlay while in-game” for each game you show (Steam, the game’s Properties).

Your pointer on the stream

On Screen Trainer the mouse is the player. Viewers see your own pointer where your mouse is: over a game it is where the mouse is in that game, anywhere else where it is on your main screen. Windows' own pointer is taken out of the games' pictures, so there is only one.

Pointer

Pointers are won in the game. The one you choose here is also the one visitors see when you visit them.

Windows' own pointer in each game

Set it per game in the games list (“mouse: hidden / shown”). Hidden is best: the little part of the game under the mouse is then shown as it was a moment before, until the mouse moves on.
