Visit another Trainer

You see their field and play on it with a pointer of your own: weaken wild Pokémon, throw balls. Whatever is caught while you are there, each of you gets one.

Looking for somebody…

Left click: weaken a wild Pokémon or pet. Right click: throw a ball. The ball on top of the fountain takes you home.

Who may visit you

A visit is a direct connection between two computers, without a server in between. As in any such game, the Trainers you meet can find out your IP address, and you theirs.

Your friend code-----·-----

Only you can type: your visitors answer with ready-made lines. What you type shows on your stream.

You

A name is a class and a number. Visitors talk with ready-made lines; a host can type to the visitors on their own stage, so when you visit, what your host writes is theirs to answer for.
