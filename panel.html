Options

How to play

Played before, on this computer or another? (Options › Save data makes one.)

Screen Trainer is an invisible layer over your whole screen. There is no background, floor or scenery, and clicks pass straight through to your other windows unless the pointer is on a Pokémon or an item. If you would rather keep everyone in one place, Options can put up a field in a window of its own.

Catching

  • Wild Pokémon hop in from the edges of the screen. Left-click one to weaken it. Your clicks never knock it out.
  • Right-click it, or click the ball beside its name, to throw a ball. The blue bar is your catch chance: it rises as health drops and when the Pokémon is asleep, frozen, paralysed, poisoned or burned.
  • Your first catch becomes your team leader. The team holds six; later catches go to the Box.
  • The plain Poké Ball is your standard ball and never runs out. Better balls are found, 10 to 100 at a time (of the Master Ball, 1 to 10): a stack you find is used for your next throws until it is gone, then it is back to the Poké Ball. The Bag lets you choose another stack or stop using one.
  • Turn on Hands off my target in Options and the team leaves the Pokémon you are clicking completely alone.

Wild Pokémon that stay

  • A wild Pokémon that nobody is fighting settles in and strolls about instead of leaving, and some come in peace from the start. The team leaves them alone. One the team weakened for you keeps waiting to be caught.
  • Click one and it is an encounter again; point at it to see its name. Options sets how many may stay (none: they wander off as they used to).

The field (windowed mode)

  • Options or the tray menu puts up the field: a window you can move and resize. Your whole team is called inside, and wild Pokémon come in over its edges instead of the edges of the screen.
  • The field is grown from a seed, and the seed is what you have caught. With nothing caught it is plain grass; every species adds a patch of its own ground, a pond for a Water type, snow for an Ice type and so on.
  • Closing the field (or minimising it) only takes the barriers away: everyone roams the whole screen until you create it again.

Your team

  • Click a team member to pet it. Drag it to move it. Right-click it to open this window.
  • Petting an injured team member mends it a very little, until a second after the last pet.
  • Affection makes a Pokémon listen better and, at higher hearts, earn more experience, hang on at 1 HP, dodge, shrug off status and land more critical hits.
  • The leader picks the target; each member then decides whether to follow orders. Obedient members hold back so you can catch. Disobedient ones loaf, pick their own fights and will happily knock out the Pokémon you wanted.
  • Nature decides aptitude: born leaders, steady followers, and free spirits who lead poorly and listen less.

Sizes

  • Species are drawn to scale from their Pokédex height, so an Onix towers over a Pikachu. Options has gentler settings.
  • Every Pokémon is also Small, Medium, Large or Extra large for its kind (about 18, 61, 16 and 5 in a hundred). Alphas are always Extra large. Its size and height are on its team page.

Weather and legendary visits

  • Now and then the weather turns. It strengthens and weakens moves and can leave a weather mark on what you catch, but it hurts nobody. Options sets how often.
  • Rarely a legendary Pokémon visits. A Friendly one is petted, not fought: click it to earn its trust, which heals your team, earns a gift and makes it far easier to catch. A Raging one is a boss that goes for your team. A Swift one darts away from your pointer until it is out of breath. All three can be caught.

Mewtwo

The XXL Super Alpha Mewtwo on your screen is your computer's guardian. Click it, or the mewtwo key, for its window: Windows security and updates at a glance, tune-up profiles you can undo, and questions to Mewtwo, answered by itself or by an AI you connect. Drag it to move it; Options hides it.

Items

  • Now and then a beaten or caught Pokémon leaves an item behind. Click it, or wait: a free team member will fetch it.
  • Open the Bag to use medicine, give a Pokémon something to hold, use an evolution item or choose which stack of balls your next throws use.
  • During fights the team shares medicine from the Bag by itself. You can turn that off in Options.

Box

Pull the box tab on the right of the device and a second screen slides out. Drag Pokémon between the team rail and the Box, or within either, to arrange them.

Sound

The mute key sits by the speaker. random jumps to another track. During fights the music changes to a battle theme. Options has the volumes and the one-time import of sounds from a ROM.

Options

Every category on the Options screen folds shut with a click on its heading; Fold all and Open all are at the top. Experience sets how fast the team levels up, and Sprite size now goes down to 0.1×.

Android app

Options > Android app > Export APK packs the game into an app for a phone or tablet, with the options exactly as they are set at that moment. In the app they are locked, and the game plays on the field. Copy the file to the device and open it there to install. It also offers itself as a screen saver in the device's settings.

Tray icon

Right-click the tray icon for Find my team, the field window, strolling mode, size, opacity, mute, music and close. Ctrl Alt P hides or shows the overlay.
